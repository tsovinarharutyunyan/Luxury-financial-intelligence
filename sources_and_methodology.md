## Comparability
- Revenue and recurring operating income (or LVMH profit from recurring operations) are compared at group level. Definitions and business mixes are not perfectly identical.
- FY2024 Kering uses the 2025 publication's restated 2024 figures (revenue €16,874m; recurring operating income €2,440m). The older 2024 publication reported €17,194m and €2,554m before this restatement.
- Hermès 2024–2025; Kering 2024–2025; LVMH 2023–2025. No missing company-years are filled or extrapolated.
- Segment results exclude LVMH corporate and elimination entries; do not sum segment margins.
- Operating-profit bridge: ΔP=(R1−R0)×m0 + R1×(m1−m0), where m=P/R. This exact identity distinguishes scale and margin mathematically, **not** price, volume, FX, acquisitions or causal factors.
- Scenario assumptions: revenue changes −10%, −5%, 0%, +5%, +10%; operating margin changes −3, 0, +3 percentage points; independent shocks, all else held constant. These are illustrations, not company forecasts.
- Net income figures vary in reporting scope and are not central to the cross-company benchmark.
- The underlying input company/segment CSVs are sourced from the original verified project and checked against official releases listed above.
