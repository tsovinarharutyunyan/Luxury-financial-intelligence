# Investment Committee Brief | Luxury operating resilience
**Scope:** Hermès, LVMH, Kering · FY2025 results and selected history · Operational performance, not equity valuation

## Executive judgment
Hermès leads this peer group on FY2025 recurring operating margin (41.05%), compared with LVMH (21.97%) and Kering (11.11%). LVMH remains much larger in revenue, and its Fashion & Leather Goods segment generates the bulk of segment-level recurring operating profit. Kering faces the steepest apparent operating pressure among the three on the comparable 2024–2025 numbers.

## What drives the distinction?
The available financial statements establish **differences in financial outcomes**, but do not identify how much comes from price increases versus volume, FX, channel mix, or brand-specific demand. The revenue/margin bridge is therefore deliberately described as an accounting decomposition, not an economic causal model.

## Decision implications
1. **Hermès:** investigate whether its margin resilience persists through demand slowdowns; protect capacity and brand exclusivity rather than assuming perpetual expansion.
2. **LVMH:** assess Fashion & Leather Goods earnings concentration and whether retail diversification offsets softness in the core segment.
3. **Kering:** prioritize comparable profitability recovery and brand execution; track whether restructuring improves margin without accelerating revenue declines.

## Risks and next evidence
A serious investment recommendation would also require valuations, capital structure, cash conversion, regional mix, inventory and forward guidance. The current project does **not** rank stocks or make price targets.
